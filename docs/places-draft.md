# Places: merged list (owner's list + first draft)

Status: **draft, not final.** Nothing here is in the app yet. On 13 November 2026 the owner sent their own list and asked to merge it with the first draft. Each approved place will get a researched description, its location from map data, walking distance computed from coordinates, and transport information with a "last checked" date.

- **Names:** standard English spelling first, the owner's spelling in brackets, e.g. "Jabal Thawr (Jabal Tsur)". Easy to swap if the owner prefers theirs first.
- **Opening map:** shows Makkah and Madinah only. Taif and Badr are reached by zooming out or dragging the flat regional map.
- **Marks:** ★ = on the owner's list. Places without ★ came from the first draft (travel guides, or the City Sightseeing bus poster).
- **Sources:** Quran and hadith citations come from the Quran and Hadith tools with their links. Travel guides were used only to choose candidates, not as authorities. "Source needed" means nothing has been verified yet.
- Scholars differ on visiting sites beyond the mosques and cemeteries named in hadith. The app describes places factually and claims no reward that is not narrated. Wording to be decided by the owner.

Taif rose sources found so far (not yet verified): the harvest is in spring, starting late March or early April and lasting about 45 to 60 days ([Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/1836611/saudi-arabia)). For the Al Gadhi Rose Factory, one source gives early March to the end of April for visits ([Wego](https://blog.wego.com/taif-rose-festival/)), while [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/saudi-arabia/hejaz/taif/attractions/al-gadhi-rose-factory/a/poi-sig/1306905/361140) gives May to July.
