# Places: draft list for the owner's approval

Status: **draft, not approved.** Nothing here is in the app yet. The owner removes or adds places; then each approved place gets a researched description, its location from map data, walking distance computed from coordinates, and transport information with a "last checked" date.

How the list was made: the places that recur across several pilgrim guides (web search, 11 November 2026, listed at the end), with a hadith citation where one ties the place to the Prophet ﷺ. These guides are commercial and were not treated as authorities: they only decided which places to *consider*. No Saudi government list was found.

Scholars differ on visiting sites other than the mosques and cemeteries named in hadith (some discourage seeking blessing at historical sites). The app should describe places factually and not claim rewards that are not narrated. How to word this is the owner's decision.
